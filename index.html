How to play

Three moves, one picture

Every puzzle is the same simple loop — the challenge is the order you find.

1

Swipe a tile

Swipe a tile up, down, left or right to swap it with the neighbour beside it. Every tile can move — no empty space.

⇄
2

Rebuild the photo

Keep sliding until the scrambled tiles line up into the original image. A hint briefly shows the full picture if you're stuck.

3

Watch it reveal

Place the last tile and the finished photo animates in whole — then your time and moves.

What's inside

Made for calm, private play

Snap a photo, get a puzzle

Point your camera, capture a shot, and Tessera turns it into a sliding puzzle on the spot.

Or import from your gallery

Prefer a photo you already have? Pick any image from your device and puzzle it up instead.

Pick your difficulty

Easy, Medium, or Hard — larger grids for a longer solve. Each level has its own colour.

Hints, on your terms

Peek at the full image for a moment when you're stuck. The clock keeps running — use them wisely.

Satisfying reveal

The completed photo animates in whole before the results — the payoff for every solve.

Comfort built in

Dark mode, reduced-motion support, adaptive layouts, and toggleable sound and haptics.

Nothing leaves the phone

No internet permission at all. Your photos and progress stay entirely on your device.

Privacy at a glance

Fully offline. Truly private.

Tessera has no internet permission, no analytics, no ads, and no accounts. Photos you use for custom puzzles never leave your device — because they can't.
