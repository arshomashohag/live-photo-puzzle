# Privacy Policy — Tessera

_Last updated: 2026-08-12_

Tessera ("the app") is a photo puzzle game. This policy explains what the app
does and does not do with your information. In short: **the app collects no
personal data, connects to no servers, and nothing you do in the app ever
leaves your device.**

## Data we collect

**None.** Tessera does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The
app has **no internet permission**, contains no analytics, advertising, or
crash-reporting SDKs, and has no user accounts.

## Photos and the camera

To create a custom puzzle, you may capture a photo with your camera or import an
existing photo. These images are:

- used only to generate your puzzle,
- stored **only on your device** in the app's private storage,
- never uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere.

The camera is used solely at the moment you choose to capture a puzzle photo.

## On-device storage

The app stores the following **locally on your device only**:

- your settings and preferences (e.g. dark mode, sound, haptics),
- custom puzzles you create, including their images.

None of this is backed up to us or transmitted off the device by the app.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to capture a photo for a custom puzzle (only when you choose to).
- **Vibrate** — to provide haptic feedback, which you can turn off in settings.

## Deleting your data

Because everything is stored locally, you control it entirely:

- delete individual custom puzzles within the app, or
- **uninstall the app** to remove all app data from your device.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the
updated policy will be published with the app.
